Textbook

Read in order if you are new to quantum computing. Projects are introduced after the scientific material needed to understand them.

Chapter 1 — Mathematical Language of Quantum Computing
Complex amplitudes and probability, Vectors, normalization, and inner products, Dirac notation: kets and bras…
Chapter 2 — Qubits, Measurement, and Single-Qubit Gates
Measurement as sampling, X, Y, Z and H gates, Unitary evolution…
Chapter 3 — Multiple Qubits, Tensor Products, and Entanglement
Tensor products, Computational basis for n qubits, Controlled gates…
Chapter 4 — Circuits, Compilation, and Real Hardware
Logical versus physical circuits, Native gate sets, Connectivity graphs…
Chapter 5 — Interference and Quantum Search
Constructive/destructive interference, Oracles, Phase marking…
Chapter 6 — Phase, Eigenvalues, and Phase Estimation
Eigenvectors/eigenvalues, Controlled unitary operations, Phase kickback…
Chapter 7 — Noise, Error Detection, and Quantum Error Correction
Bit/phase errors, Why copying an unknown qubit is forbidden, Redundancy without cloning…
Chapter 8 — Hamiltonians, Dynamics, and Trotterization
Hamiltonians as generators of time evolution, Pauli operators, Ising interactions…
Chapter 9 — Variational and Hybrid Quantum Algorithms
Expectation values, Parameterized circuits, Classical optimizers…
Chapter 10 — Quantum Machine Learning
Data encoding, Parameterized quantum models, Measurement as model output…
Chapter 11 — Quantum Communication and BB84
Complementary bases, No-cloning principle, Prepare-and-measure protocols…
Chapter 12 — Benchmarking and Reproducible Quantum Experiments
What a benchmark can and cannot claim, Experimental controls, Raw data and metadata…
