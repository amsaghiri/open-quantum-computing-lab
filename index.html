About this educational site. This website is designed for teaching and learning quantum computing. It does not aim to reproduce materials from other educational platforms. Students may use any suitable quantum simulator or computing environment. We thank qBraid QUEST for supporting quantum computing education at William Paterson University this semester. Some supplementary references and activities have been aligned with QUEST resources to support course assignments, while OQCL remains platform-neutral and students are free to choose other suitable tools.
